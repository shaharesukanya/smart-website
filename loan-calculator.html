Understand Your Loan Calculation

Learn how the Loan Calculator estimates your monthly payment, interest and total repayment.

What is a Loan Calculator?

A loan calculator is an online tool that helps estimate the regular payment required to repay a loan based on the loan amount, interest rate and repayment period.

It can help you understand the relationship between the amount borrowed, interest and repayment period before making financial decisions.

How to Use the Loan Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate.
  3. Enter the loan tenure in years.
  4. Click the "Calculate Loan" button.
  5. View monthly payment, interest and total payment.

Loan Payment Formula

For a standard reducing-balance loan, the monthly payment can be estimated using:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

P = principal loan amount, r = monthly interest rate, n = total number of monthly payments.
