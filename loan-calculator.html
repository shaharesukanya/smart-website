What is a Loan Calculator?

A loan calculator is an online tool that helps estimate the regular payment required to repay a loan based on the loan amount, interest rate and repayment period.

How Does a Loan Calculator Work?

The calculator uses the loan amount, annual interest rate and loan tenure to estimate the monthly payment.

The calculation also estimates the total amount paid and the total interest over the selected loan period.

How to Use the Loan Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate.
  3. Enter the loan tenure in years.
  4. Click the "Calculate Loan" button.
  5. View the estimated monthly payment, total interest and total payment.

Loan Calculator vs EMI Calculator

A loan calculator and an EMI calculator can use similar loan information and formulas. The terminology may differ depending on the type of loan and the calculation being performed.

Important Information

The result provided by this calculator is an estimate. Actual loan payments may vary depending on lender terms, processing fees, taxes, insurance, charges and other conditions.
