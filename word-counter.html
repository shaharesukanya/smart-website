What is a Word Counter?

A word counter is an online writing tool that quickly measures the number of words in your text. SmartTools also provides character, sentence and line counts so you can understand your content at a glance.

Useful for

  • Essays and assignments
  • Blog posts and articles
  • Social media content
  • Applications and cover letters
  • SEO content writing

How counting works

Words are detected by separating non-empty text sections around whitespace. Characters include spaces and punctuation, while the separate character count ignores spaces.

Reading time

Reading time is estimated using approximately 200 words per minute. The displayed value is an estimate and can vary depending on reading speed and the type of content.
