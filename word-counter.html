What is a Word Counter?

A word counter is an online tool that counts the number of words in a piece of text.

It can be useful for students, writers, bloggers, content creators and professionals who need to meet specific word-count limits.

How Does the Word Counter Work?

The tool checks the text you enter and separates words based on spaces and other whitespace characters.

How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. Click the "Count Words" button.
  3. Your total word count will be displayed.
