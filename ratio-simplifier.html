Ratio Simplifier Formula

Understanding the calculation makes ratios easier to work with.

Greatest Common Factor

First, find the greatest common factor (GCF) of the two numbers. Then divide both values by that factor.

Simplified Ratio = A ÷ GCF : B ÷ GCF

Example

Suppose the ratio is 18 : 24.

GCF of 18 and 24 = 6
18 ÷ 6 = 3
24 ÷ 6 = 4

Final Ratio = 3 : 4
