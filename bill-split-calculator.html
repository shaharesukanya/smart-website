What is a Bill Split Calculator?

A bill split calculator helps divide a shared bill between multiple people. You can optionally include a tip percentage before dividing the total.

Tip Formula

Tip = Bill × Tip % ÷ 100

Final Bill

Final Bill = Bill + Tip

Amount Per Person

Per Person = Final Bill ÷ People

Example

A ₹2,500 bill with a 10% tip becomes ₹2,750. If 5 people share it equally, each person pays ₹550.
