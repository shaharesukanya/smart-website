What is Compound Interest?

Compound interest is interest calculated on the original principal as well as interest accumulated during previous periods.

Because interest can be added back to the balance, the amount can grow over time.

Compound Interest Formula

A = P × (1 + r/n)nt

Where A is the final amount, P is the principal, r is the annual interest rate in decimal, n is the number of compounding periods per year, and t is time in years.

Compound Interest

Compound Interest = A − P

Example

Suppose ₹1,00,000 is invested at 8% annual interest for 5 years with quarterly compounding. The calculator uses the compound interest formula to estimate the final amount and interest earned.
