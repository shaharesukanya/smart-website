Time Duration Formula

The calculator converts both times to seconds before finding their difference.

Basic Formula

Convert the start and end times into total seconds, then subtract the start from the end.

Duration = End Time − Start Time

Seconds Conversion

1 hour = 3,600 seconds
1 minute = 60 seconds

Example: Same Day

Start: 9:00 AM

End: 11:30 AM

Duration: 2 hours 30 minutes

Example: Overnight

Start: 10:00 PM

End: 2:00 AM

Next-day option enabled: 4 hours

How to Use the Time Duration Calculator

Find the difference between two times in a few simple steps.

Step 1 — Select Start Time

Choose the time when the activity begins.

Step 2 — Select End Time

Choose the time when the activity ends.

Step 3 — Handle Overnight Time

Enable the next-day option when the end time occurs after midnight.

Step 4 — Calculate

Click Calculate Duration to see hours, minutes and seconds.
