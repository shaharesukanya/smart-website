What is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator helps you calculate how much money you save when a percentage discount is applied to an original price.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

If the original price is ₹2,000 and the discount is 20%:

Discount = ₹2,000 × 20 ÷ 100 = ₹400

Final Price = ₹2,000 − ₹400 = ₹1,600

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Click "Calculate Discount".
  4. Check your discount amount and final price.
