What is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator helps you calculate how much money you save when a discount is applied to an original price. It also calculates the final price you need to pay after subtracting the discount.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount % ÷ 100

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

How to Use the Discount Calculator

  1. Enter the original price of the product.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Click Calculate Discount.
  4. View the discount amount and final price.

Example Discount Calculation

Suppose a product costs ₹1,000 and has a 20% discount.

Discount = ₹1,000 × 20 ÷ 100
Discount = ₹200

Final Price = ₹1,000 − ₹200
Final Price = ₹800

Where Can You Use a Discount Calculator?

You can use a discount calculator for shopping, sale prices, product comparisons, online purchases and everyday price calculations.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a discount?
Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100. The result is the discount amount.
How do I calculate the final price after discount?
Subtract the discount amount from the original price.
What is 20% off ₹1,000?
A 20% discount on ₹1,000 is ₹200. The final price is ₹800.
Can I calculate a 50% discount?
Yes. Enter 50 as the discount percentage.
Can I use this calculator on mobile?
Yes. The calculator is responsive and works on modern mobile, tablet and desktop browsers.
