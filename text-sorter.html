How to Use the Text Sorter

Organize your text in a few simple steps.

Step 1 — Paste Your Text

Add your list or text to the input box. Each line is handled as a separate item.

Step 2 — Choose Sort Order

Select alphabetical, numerical or text-length sorting.

Step 3 — Choose Cleanup Options

You can remove duplicate lines, clean spaces and ignore letter case during sorting.

Step 4 — Sort

Click Sort Text to generate the organized output instantly.
