What is a URL Slug?

A URL slug is the readable part of a web address that identifies a specific page. For example, /best-web-design-tools is a slug.


Clean slugs can make URLs easier to read, share and understand. They are commonly used for blog posts, articles, product pages and documentation.

Why use a slug generator?

  • Creates readable URLs quickly
  • Removes unnecessary special characters
  • Converts spaces into URL-friendly separators
  • Helps maintain consistent URL formatting
  • Useful for blogs, websites and developer projects

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an SEO-friendly slug?

An SEO-friendly slug is a short, readable URL segment that clearly describes the page content.

Should URL slugs use hyphens?

Hyphens are commonly used to separate words in readable URLs.

Should a slug be lowercase?

Lowercase slugs are commonly used because they provide a consistent and clean URL format.

Can I use this tool for blog URLs?

Yes. You can convert blog titles, article titles, product names and other text into URL slugs.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No server is required. The conversion happens directly inside your browser.
