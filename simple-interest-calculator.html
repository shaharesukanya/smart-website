What is Simple Interest?

Simple interest is interest calculated on the original principal amount for a specified period.

It is commonly used for straightforward interest calculations where interest does not compound.

Simple Interest Formula

SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100

Total Amount Formula

A = P + SI

Example

For ₹1,00,000 at 8% per year for 5 years, simple interest is ₹40,000 and the total amount is ₹1,40,000.

How It Works

P = Principal amount

R = Annual interest rate

T = Time in years

SI = Simple interest

A = Total amount

Important Note

Actual interest terms may differ by lender, financial product or agreement. Use the result as a calculation estimate.
