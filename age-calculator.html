How Age Is Calculated

The calculator compares your date of birth with today's date and calculates the calendar difference in complete years, months and days.

Age = Current Date − Date of Birth

The calculation accounts for different month lengths and leap years when determining the remaining months and days.

How to Use

  1. Select your date of birth.
  2. Click Calculate My Age.
  3. Your age appears in years, months and days.
  4. Check your next birthday information.
  5. Use the result for your everyday calculations.
