What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that calculates a person's age based on their date of birth.

Instead of manually calculating the difference between two dates, you can enter your date of birth and get your age quickly.

How to Use the Age Calculator

Using the SmartTools Age Calculator is simple:

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Click the "Calculate Age" button.
  3. Your age will be displayed in years, months and days.

What Does the Result Show?

The calculator shows your approximate current age as a combination of years, months and days.

For example, the result may look like:

25 Years, 4 Months, 12 Days
