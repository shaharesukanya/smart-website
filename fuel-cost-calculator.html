What is a Fuel Cost Calculator?

A fuel cost calculator estimates the amount of fuel needed for a journey based on distance and vehicle mileage. It then multiplies the required fuel by the fuel price to estimate the trip cost.

Fuel Required Formula

Fuel Required = Distance ÷ Mileage

Fuel Cost Formula

Fuel Cost = Fuel Required × Fuel Price

Example

For a 300 km trip, a vehicle giving 18 km/l would require approximately 16.67 litres of fuel. At ₹100 per litre, the estimated fuel cost would be approximately ₹1,666.67.

How to Get a Better Estimate

Use the vehicle's realistic average mileage rather than its maximum advertised mileage.

Enter the fuel price applicable to the trip.

Include the full journey distance when calculating a return trip.

Important Note

Actual fuel consumption can vary because of traffic, road conditions, driving style, vehicle load, weather and other factors.
