FAQ

Questions, answered.

Quick information about how SmartTools works and what you can expect from the platform.

Are SmartTools tools free to use?

Yes. The tools on this website are designed to be available directly through your browser.

Do I need to install software?

No installation is required for the browser-based tools. Open a tool, enter or upload your data where supported, and use it.

Can I use SmartTools on mobile?

Yes. The website uses responsive layouts designed to work across modern phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Are uploaded files processed in the browser?

Many data and conversion tools are designed for client-side browser processing. Each individual tool explains its own processing behavior.

Who created SmartTools?

SmartTools is created and developed by Sukanya Shahare.
