Remove Duplicate Lines Online

The SmartTools Duplicate Line Remover helps you clean lists, copied data, notes, names, URLs, keywords and other text containing repeated lines.

How it works

The tool checks every line of your text and keeps the first occurrence of each unique line. Repeated lines are removed while the original order is preserved.

Useful for

  • Cleaning copied lists
  • Removing repeated names or items
  • Cleaning keyword lists
  • Organizing URLs or text data
  • Preparing text for spreadsheets
  • Cleaning notes and everyday text

Quick Tips

For the cleanest result, paste one item per line.

Example

Apple
Banana
Apple
Mango

Result:

Apple
Banana
Mango
