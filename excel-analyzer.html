What is an Excel Analyzer?

An Excel Analyzer is a spreadsheet analysis tool that helps you understand the structure and quality of your Excel data. It can show the number of rows and columns, missing cells, duplicate records and basic statistics for individual columns.

This tool is useful for students, developers, business users, analysts and anyone who needs a quick overview of an Excel spreadsheet before performing deeper analysis.

How to analyze an Excel file?

Select an XLSX or XLS file from your computer. Choose the worksheet you want to inspect and click Analyze Sheet. SmartTools will then generate a summary and preview of your spreadsheet data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Excel Analyzer free?

Yes. You can use the analyzer directly in your browser without creating an account.

Which Excel files are supported?

The tool supports common XLSX and XLS spreadsheet files.

Are my Excel files uploaded to a server?

The analysis is performed locally in your browser. The page does not intentionally send the spreadsheet data to a SmartTools backend.

Can it detect missing values?

Yes. Empty cells are counted and shown for each column.

Can it detect duplicate rows?

Yes. The analyzer compares complete rows and reports duplicate records.
