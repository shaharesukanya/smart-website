What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the amount a borrower generally pays every month toward a loan.

An EMI normally consists of two components: the principal amount and the interest charged on the outstanding loan balance.

How Does an EMI Calculator Work?

An EMI calculator uses the loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure to estimate the monthly repayment amount.

The standard EMI calculation uses the following formula:

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + R)ⁿ − 1)

Here, P represents the principal loan amount, R represents the monthly interest rate and n represents the total number of monthly instalments.
