EMI Calculator

SmartTools EMI Calculator helps you estimate your monthly loan payment, total interest and total repayment based on the loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure.

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

P = Principal loan amount
r = Monthly interest rate
n = Total number of monthly payments

Example

For a ₹5,00,000 loan at 8.5% annual interest for 5 years, the calculator converts the annual rate into a monthly rate and uses 60 monthly payments to estimate the EMI.

How to Use the EMI Calculator

  2. Enter the annual interest rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does EMI mean?
EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the periodic amount paid toward a loan, normally including principal and interest.
Does a longer loan tenure reduce EMI?
A longer tenure generally reduces the monthly EMI but can increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.
Can this calculator show total interest?
Yes. The calculator estimates total interest using the loan amount, interest rate and tenure.
Can I use this calculator on mobile?
Yes. The page is responsive and works on modern desktop, tablet and mobile browsers.
Is the EMI result a bank quotation?
No. The result is an estimate based on the values entered into the calculator. Actual loan terms may vary by lender and loan product.
