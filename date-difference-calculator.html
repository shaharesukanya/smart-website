How Date Difference Is Calculated

The calculator uses calendar dates and calculates the elapsed time between them.

Total Days

The total number of calendar days is calculated from the start date to the end date.

Days = End Date − Start Date

Total Weeks

The day difference can be converted into weeks by dividing the total days by 7.

Weeks = Days ÷ 7

Total Months

Months are estimated from the total day difference using the average length of a calendar month.

Approx. Months = Days ÷ 30.436875

Total Years

Years are shown using an average Gregorian calendar year length.

Approx. Years = Days ÷ 365.2425
