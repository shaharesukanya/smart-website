What is an Excel to PDF Converter?

An Excel to PDF converter transforms spreadsheet information into a portable PDF document that is easier to share, print and view across different devices.

SmartTools Excel to PDF provides a simple browser-based workflow for converting a selected Excel worksheet into a PDF document.

How does Excel to PDF conversion work?

Upload your XLSX or XLS file, select a worksheet, choose the PDF orientation and click Generate PDF. The selected worksheet is then placed into a printable PDF document.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Excel to PDF free?

Yes. SmartTools provides this browser-based converter without requiring an account.

Which Excel formats are supported?

XLSX and XLS files are supported.

Can I choose a worksheet?

Yes. If your Excel file contains multiple worksheets, you can select the worksheet you want to convert.

Can I use landscape PDF?

Yes. You can select portrait or landscape orientation before generating the PDF.

Is my Excel data uploaded?

The spreadsheet is processed in your browser. The page does not intentionally send the spreadsheet data to a SmartTools backend.
