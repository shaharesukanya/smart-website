What is a Case Converter?

A case converter is an online text tool that changes the capitalization of your text. You can quickly convert text into uppercase, lowercase, title case or sentence case.

Available Text Conversions

Uppercase: Converts all letters to capital letters.

Lowercase: Converts all letters to small letters.

Title Case: Capitalizes the first letter of each word.

Sentence Case: Capitalizes the first letter of each sentence.

How to Use Case Converter?

Enter or paste your text into the text box and select one of the conversion buttons. The converted text will appear instantly below.
