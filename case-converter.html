What is a Case Converter?

A case converter changes the capitalization of text without requiring you to retype it. It is useful when formatting documents, headlines, articles, notes and other content.

Available formats

  • UPPERCASE
  • lowercase
  • Title Case
  • Sentence case
  • Alternating Case
  • Inverse Case

When should you use Title Case?

Title Case can be useful for headings and titles where important words begin with capital letters. Exact editorial capitalization rules can vary depending on the style guide.

Is the tool automatic?

Yes. The conversion happens instantly in your browser after you choose a conversion option. You can then copy the result and use it wherever you need it.
