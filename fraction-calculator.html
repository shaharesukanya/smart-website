Fraction Formulas

These are the basic rules used for fraction calculations.

Addition

For fractions with different denominators, convert them to a common denominator before adding.

a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd

Subtraction

Multiply across to create a common denominator and then subtract the numerators.

a/b − c/d = (ad − bc) / bd

Multiplication

Multiply the numerators together and multiply the denominators together.

a/b × c/d = ac/bd

Division

To divide fractions, multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second fraction.

a/b ÷ c/d = ad/bc
