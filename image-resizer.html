How to resize an image

Choose an image, enter the desired width and height, select an output format, then click Resize Image. When aspect ratio is enabled, changing one dimension automatically adjusts the other.

Why use an image resizer?

Resizing is useful when a website, form, profile, document or social platform requires a specific image dimension. Smaller dimensions can also reduce the amount of data an image needs to display.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does SmartTools upload my image?

No. The image is loaded and processed in your browser using the Canvas API.

Can I resize JPG, PNG and WebP?

Yes. The tool accepts JPG, PNG and WebP files and lets you choose the output format.

What does Keep aspect ratio do?

It keeps the original width-to-height proportion so the image is less likely to look stretched.
