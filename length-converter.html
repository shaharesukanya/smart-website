Length Conversion Basics

All supported units are converted through a common meter-based conversion system.

Metric Units

The metric system commonly uses kilometers, meters, centimeters and millimeters for measuring length and distance.

1 kilometer = 1,000 meters

Imperial Units

Miles, yards, feet and inches are commonly used in the imperial or customary measurement systems.

1 mile = 5,280 feet

Meter Conversion

SmartTools uses meters as the base unit and converts the input into meters before converting it to the selected target unit.

Converted Value = Meters ÷ Target Unit Factor

Common Example

1 km = 1,000 m

1 m = 100 cm

1 m = 1,000 mm

1 mile ≈ 1.609 km
