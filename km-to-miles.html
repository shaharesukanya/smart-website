How Many Miles is 1 Kilometer?

One kilometer is approximately equal to 0.621371 miles.

KM to Miles Formula

To convert kilometers to miles, multiply the number of kilometers by 0.621371.

Miles = Kilometers × 0.621371

Example

If the distance is 10 kilometers:

10 × 0.621371 = 6.21371 miles
