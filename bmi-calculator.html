What is BMI?

BMI, or Body Mass Index, is a number calculated from a person's height and weight. It is commonly used as one general screening measure related to body weight.

Enter your measurements above and SmartTools will calculate the BMI value instantly.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) / Height² (m)

Example

A person weighing 65 kg with a height of 1.70 m has a BMI of approximately 22.5.

BMI Categories

For adults, BMI values are commonly grouped into standard screening categories.

Underweight

BMI below 18.5

Normal range

BMI from 18.5 to 24.9

Overweight

BMI from 25.0 to 29.9

Obesity

BMI 30.0 or above

BMI is a general screening measure and is not a medical diagnosis.
