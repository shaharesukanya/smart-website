Temperature Conversion Formulas

These standard formulas are used to convert between temperature scales.

Celsius to Fahrenheit

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 and then add 32.

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Fahrenheit to Celsius

Subtract 32 from Fahrenheit and multiply by 5/9.

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

Celsius to Kelvin

Add 273.15 to the temperature in Celsius.

K = °C + 273.15

Kelvin to Celsius

Subtract 273.15 from the temperature in Kelvin.

°C = K − 273.15

Common Temperature Examples

A few useful reference points across the temperature scales.

Water Freezing Point

0°C = 32°F = 273.15 K

Water Boiling Point

100°C = 212°F = 373.15 K

Room Temperature Example

25°C = 77°F = 298.15 K

Absolute Zero

0 K = −273.15°C = −459.67°F
