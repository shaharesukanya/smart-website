What is a Percentage Calculator?

A percentage calculator is an online tool that makes common percentage calculations quick and easy. You can calculate a percentage of a number, find what percentage one number represents of another, or calculate the percentage increase or decrease between two values.

Percentage Formula

Percentage of a number:
Result = Percentage × Number ÷ 100

What percentage:
Percentage = Part ÷ Whole × 100

Percentage change:
Change % = (New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value × 100

How to Use the Percentage Calculator

  1. Choose the type of percentage calculation.
  2. Enter the required numbers.
  3. Click the Calculate Percentage button.
  4. Review the result and calculation working.

Example: What is 20% of 500?

To calculate 20% of 500:

20 × 500 ÷ 100 = 100

Therefore, 20% of 500 is 100.

Example: Percentage Increase

If a value changes from 500 to 600:

(600 − 500) ÷ 500 × 100 = 20%

The percentage increase is 20%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?
Multiply the percentage by the number and divide the result by 100. For example, 20% of 500 is 20 × 500 ÷ 100 = 100.
How do I find what percentage one number is of another?
Divide the first number by the second number and multiply by 100.
How is percentage increase calculated?
Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value and multiply by 100.
Can this calculator calculate percentage decrease?
Yes. Enter the original value and the new lower value. The calculator will show the percentage change.
Can I use this calculator on mobile?
Yes. The calculator is responsive and works on mobile, tablet and desktop browsers.
