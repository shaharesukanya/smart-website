What is a Percentage?

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. The word percentage means "per hundred".

Percentages are commonly used in discounts, marks, statistics, finance, business and everyday calculations.

How to Calculate a Percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, use the following basic formula:

Percentage Result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

Example

Suppose you want to calculate 20% of 500.

The calculation is:

(20 ÷ 100) × 500 = 100

Therefore, 20% of 500 is 100.
