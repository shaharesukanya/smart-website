What is an Average?

The arithmetic average, also called the arithmetic mean, is calculated by adding all values and dividing the total by the number of values.

Average Formula

Average = Sum of Values ÷ Number of Values

Example

For 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50, the sum is 150. Dividing 150 by 5 gives an average of 30.
