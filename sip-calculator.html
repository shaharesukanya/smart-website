What is SIP?

SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan. It is a method of investing a fixed amount at regular intervals, commonly in mutual funds.

A SIP calculator can help estimate how an investment may grow over a selected period based on an assumed rate of return.

How Does a SIP Calculator Work?

The calculator uses your monthly investment, assumed annual rate of return and investment period to estimate the future value.

The calculation uses a monthly compounding assumption based on the annual return entered.

How to Use the SIP Calculator

  1. Enter your monthly investment amount.
  2. Enter the expected annual return.
  3. Enter the investment period in years.
  4. Click the "Calculate SIP" button.
  5. View the estimated total investment, estimated returns and future value.
