What is Pace?

Pace is the amount of time required to cover a unit of distance. For running and walking, pace is commonly expressed as minutes per kilometre or minutes per mile.

Pace Formula

Pace = Total Time ÷ Distance

Speed Formula

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

Example

If you run 5 km in 30 minutes, your average pace is 6:00 minutes per kilometre and your average speed is 10 km/h.
