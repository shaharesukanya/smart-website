Character Counter Online

SmartTools Character Counter is a simple and free online tool for counting characters in your text. It is useful for social media posts, essays, assignments, forms, descriptions and other writing tasks.

What Does This Tool Count?

This tool shows the total number of characters, characters without spaces, total words and number of lines in your text.

How to Use Character Counter?

Enter or paste your text into the text box and click the "Count Characters" button. Your results will appear immediately.
