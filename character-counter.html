What is a Character Counter?

A character counter measures how many characters are present in your text. SmartTools shows both the complete character count and the count after removing whitespace.

Why count characters?

  • Check social media text limits
  • Prepare short descriptions
  • Check application or form limits
  • Edit headlines and titles
  • Keep written content concise

Characters vs words

A word is generally a group of text separated by whitespace, while characters include letters, numbers, punctuation and spaces. One word can therefore contain many characters.

Does punctuation count?

Yes. Punctuation marks such as commas, periods, question marks and exclamation marks are included in the character count because they are characters in the text.
