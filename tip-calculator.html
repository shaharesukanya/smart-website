What is a Tip Calculator?

A tip calculator helps calculate the amount of gratuity to add to a restaurant, service or other bill based on a percentage.

You can also use it to divide the final bill between multiple people.

Tip Formula

Tip = Bill Amount × Tip % ÷ 100

Total Bill Formula

Total Bill = Bill + Tip

Per Person Formula

Per Person = Total Bill ÷ Number of People

Example

For a ₹2,500 bill with a 10% tip, the tip is ₹250 and the total is ₹2,750. If 5 people share the bill, each person pays ₹550.
