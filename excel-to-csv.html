What is Excel to CSV?

Excel to CSV conversion transforms spreadsheet data into comma-separated values. CSV is a simple text-based format that is supported by databases, spreadsheets, programming languages and many data-analysis applications.

Why convert Excel to CSV?

CSV files are lightweight and easy to process. Developers and data analysts commonly use CSV files when moving data between different applications and systems.

Multiple worksheets

Excel workbooks can contain multiple worksheets. This tool lets you select the worksheet you want before converting it.

Browser-based processing

The conversion happens directly inside your browser. Your spreadsheet does not need to be uploaded to a SmartTools server for conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert XLSX to CSV?

Yes. XLSX files are supported.

Can I convert XLS files?

Yes. Older XLS spreadsheets are also supported.

Can I select a specific worksheet?

Yes. If your workbook has multiple sheets, select the required worksheet from the worksheet dropdown.

Can I change the CSV separator?

Yes. You can use comma, semicolon, pipe or tab as the CSV separator.

Can I download the converted CSV?

Yes. Click Download CSV after conversion to save the generated file.

Is my Excel file uploaded?

No. The conversion is performed locally in your browser.
