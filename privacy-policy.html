Privacy Policy

This page explains how SmartTools approaches information and privacy when you use our website, calculators, converters and productivity tools.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. Introduction

SmartTools is a collection of online calculators, converters and productivity tools created and maintained by Sukanya Shahare.

We aim to keep the website simple, useful and respectful of user privacy. This Privacy Policy describes the general types of information that may be handled when you visit or use SmartTools.

2. Information You Provide

Some SmartTools pages may allow you to voluntarily provide information, such as when you contact us.

Depending on how you contact us, this may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The subject and content of your message
  • Any other information you choose to include

3. Calculator and Tool Inputs

SmartTools calculators and productivity tools may require you to enter information in order to produce a result.

Examples may include dates, numbers, text, financial values or conversion inputs.

Whenever possible, tool calculations are designed to be performed within your browser. You should avoid entering sensitive personal, financial, password or other confidential information into public website tools.

4. Automatically Collected Information

Like many websites, basic technical information may be processed by the hosting platform or other services used to operate the website.

Depending on the services enabled on the website, this may include information such as device type, browser type, approximate usage information, referring pages and technical request data.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

SmartTools may use cookies or similar technologies where needed for website functionality, preferences, analytics or other supported services.

Your browser may provide controls that allow you to manage or restrict cookies.

6. Third-Party Services

SmartTools may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, security, performance monitoring or other website functionality.

Those services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

7. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to messages and enquiries
  • Improve website content and usability
  • Understand technical problems
  • Maintain website functionality and security
  • Communicate when necessary about a request

8. Data Sharing

SmartTools does not intend to sell personal information simply because you use the website.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website, respond to requests, maintain security or comply with applicable obligations.

9. External Links

SmartTools may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

Review the privacy policy of any external website before providing information to it.

10. Children's Privacy

SmartTools is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed at collecting personal information from children.

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of online services where appropriate.

11. Security

Reasonable efforts may be used to protect website information and maintain secure services. However, no internet transmission or online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, features or services change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent through the SmartTools Contact page.
