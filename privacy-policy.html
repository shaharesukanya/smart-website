Privacy Policy

Information about privacy and data handling at SmartTools.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SmartTools. SmartTools provides free online calculators, converters and text tools for general informational and utility purposes.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use our website.

2. Information We Collect

SmartTools is designed so that many of its calculators, converters and text tools can be used without creating an account or providing personal information.

Information may be collected automatically by standard website technologies such as cookies, analytics services or advertising services, if these services are enabled on the website.

3. Information You Enter

Some SmartTools pages allow you to enter numbers or text into calculators and text tools.

You should avoid entering sensitive personal information, passwords, financial account details or other confidential information into public website tools.

4. Cookies

SmartTools may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website usage and support advertising or analytics services.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Analytics

If analytics services are used, they may collect information such as pages visited, approximate device information, browser type, referring pages and general usage patterns.

Analytics information is used to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content and functionality.

6. Advertising

SmartTools may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers in the future.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements according to their own policies.

7. Third-Party Websites

SmartTools may contain links to third-party websites or services.

SmartTools is not responsible for the privacy practices, content or policies of external websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

SmartTools is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed toward collecting personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website, services or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or SmartTools, please contact us through the contact information provided on the website.
