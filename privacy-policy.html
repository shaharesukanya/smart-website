🔒 Privacy & Transparency

Privacy Policy.

This page explains how SmartTools approaches information, browser-based tools, cookies and third-party services used by the website.

Last updated: September 2026
SmartTools is a collection of online calculators, converters and productivity utilities. We aim to keep our tools simple and transparent about how information may be handled. This policy may be updated as the website's features and services change.

1. Information You Provide

Some parts of the website may allow you to voluntarily provide information, such as when using a contact form or sending feedback.

Only provide information that is appropriate for the purpose of your message. Do not submit passwords, financial account credentials, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information through a general contact form.

2. Browser-Based Tools

Many SmartTools calculators and utilities are designed to perform their calculations directly in your web browser.

For example, values entered into a calculator may be processed by JavaScript running on your device rather than being sent to a SmartTools server.

This does not mean every future SmartTools feature will necessarily work this way. Features may change as the website develops.

3. Automatically Collected Information

Depending on the hosting environment, analytics, security services or other technologies used on the website, certain technical information may potentially be processed automatically.

This can include information such as browser type, device information, approximate geographic region, referring pages and basic usage information.

The exact information collected depends on the services enabled on the website at a particular time.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

SmartTools may use cookies or similar technologies where needed for website functionality, preferences, analytics or third-party services.

Your browser may allow you to control or block cookies through its settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

5. Third-Party Services

SmartTools may use third-party services to support hosting, analytics, advertising, security or other website functionality.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

SmartTools does not control the privacy practices of external services that operate independently from this website.

6. Links to Other Websites

SmartTools may contain links to external websites or services.

Once you leave SmartTools and visit another website, that website's own privacy policy and terms apply.

7. Children's Privacy

SmartTools is intended to provide general-purpose online tools and information. We do not knowingly request sensitive personal information from children.

8. Data Security

Reasonable care may be taken to protect information handled through the website. However, no method of transmission or storage on the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices

Depending on the feature or service involved, you may be able to control information by:

  • Choosing whether to submit information through optional forms.
  • Managing cookies through your browser settings.
  • Disabling or limiting certain browser features.
  • Choosing not to use third-party services linked from the website.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

SmartTools may update this Privacy Policy when the website, tools, technology or services change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

For questions or feedback about this Privacy Policy, please visit the Contact page .

12. Important Disclaimer

SmartTools provides general-purpose online tools and information. Results from calculators should be treated as estimates or informational outputs unless a tool specifically states otherwise.

For financial, tax, legal, medical or other important decisions, verify information with an appropriate qualified or official source.
