GST Calculator

SmartTools GST Calculator helps you quickly calculate the GST amount and the final amount after GST is added.

GST Amount = Original Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

Final Amount = Original Amount + GST Amount

Example

Suppose the original amount is ₹1,000 and the GST rate is 18%.

GST Amount: ₹1,000 × 18 ÷ 100 = ₹180

Final Amount: ₹1,000 + ₹180 = ₹1,180

How to Use the GST Calculator

  1. Enter the original amount.
  2. Select the applicable GST rate.
  3. Click the Calculate GST button.
  4. View the GST amount and final amount.
