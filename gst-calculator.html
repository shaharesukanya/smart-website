What is GST?

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax. It is a consumption-based tax applied to the supply of goods and services.

How is GST Calculated?

When GST is added to a price, the basic calculation is:

GST Amount = Original Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

The final amount after adding GST is:

Final Amount = Original Amount + GST Amount

Example

Suppose the original amount is ₹1,000 and the GST rate is 18%.

GST amount:

₹1,000 × 18 ÷ 100 = ₹180

Final amount:

₹1,000 + ₹180 = ₹1,180

Common GST Rates

Depending on the applicable goods or services, different GST rates may apply. Common rates include 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%.

The applicable rate depends on the relevant goods or services and current tax rules.
