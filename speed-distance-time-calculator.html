Speed Distance Time Formula

Speed, distance and time are connected through a simple mathematical relationship.

Speed Formula

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

Distance Formula

Distance = Speed × Time

Time Formula

Time = Distance ÷ Speed

Example

If a vehicle travels 120 km in 2 hours, its average speed is 60 km/h.
