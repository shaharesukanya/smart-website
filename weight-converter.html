Weight Conversion Basics

SmartTools uses kilograms as the base unit for the conversions.

Metric Units

Kilograms, grams, milligrams and tonnes are commonly used metric units for measuring mass.

1 kg = 1,000 g

Imperial Units

Pounds and ounces are commonly used in imperial and US customary measurements.

1 pound = 16 ounces

Common Conversions

1 tonne = 1,000 kg

1 kg = 1,000 g

1 g = 1,000 mg

1 kg ≈ 2.20462 lb

Example

Suppose you have 5 kilograms and want to convert it to grams.

5 × 1,000 = 5,000 g
