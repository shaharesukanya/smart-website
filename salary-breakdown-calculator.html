What is a Salary Breakdown?

A salary breakdown shows how annual CTC may be distributed across salary components and deductions. CTC and monthly take-home salary are not necessarily the same.

Basic Salary Estimate

Basic Salary = CTC × Basic Salary %

Employee PF Estimate

PF = Basic Salary × PF %

Approximate Annual In-Hand

Annual In-Hand = CTC − Estimated Deductions

Why Can Actual Salary Differ?

Different employers may use different salary structures. CTC can include bonuses, employer contributions, insurance, gratuity and other components.

Important Note

Actual income tax, PF treatment, professional tax, benefits and other payroll rules can affect your final payslip.

Use this calculator as an estimate rather than an exact payroll calculation.
